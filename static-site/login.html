University of Delhi Undergraduate Admissions 2026
  1. Admission to all Under-Graduate Programs of UoD for the Academic Session 2026-27 will be through CSAS(UG)-2026, based on the eligibility requirements stated in UG BOI-2026 and other rules as published by the University of Delhi on its website.
  2. Admission to all UG Programs of UoD will be done through this portal on the basis of the Common University Entrance Test- Under-Graduate 2026 (CUET (UG)  2026).
  3. The Common Seat Allocation System (UG) 2026 includes:

    4. PHASE I: APPLYING TO UNIVERSITY OF DELHI

    PHASE II: FILLING THE PREFERENCES FOR PROGRAMS AND COLLEGES

    PHASE III: ALLOCATION-CUM-ADMISSION

  4. There is one-time CSAS(UG)-2026 Application fee  (non-refundable)

    5.  Category  

                  CSAS(UG)-2026 Application Fee  

     UR/OBC-NCL/EWS  

            Rs. 250.00 (Rupees Two Hundred Fifty only)  

     SC/ST/PwBD  

           Rs. 100.00 (Rupees Hundred only)  

    Candidates applying for BFA/ B. Sc(PE,HE &S) / B.A.(H) Music will have to pay an additional fee of Rs. 400.00 (non-refundable).

    For applying to ECA and Sports supernumerary quota, there is an additional fee of Rs.100.00 (Rupees Hundred only) for each quota.

  5. The online application process will be considered completed only after the realization of the CSAS(UG)-2026 Application fee
  6. Candidates must check their dashboard, email, and admission website ( www.admission.uod.ac.in) for all communications, updates and schedules related to all the Phases of CSAS(UG)-2026.
