Disclaimer: Selection of a Supernumerary Quota

Before opting for the allocation on the Supernumerary quota, you should note:

  1. As per the University of Delhi’s allocation policy, candidates are allowed to take admission on only one seat at a time. Thus, you cannot hold the seat allocated to you through regular rounds (if allocated) and of Supernumerary quota simultaneously.
  2. If you choose to accept a seat allocated under the Supernumerary Quota, you are consenting to be considered only on the Supernumerary quota in the subsequent round/s, if any, subject to the allocation rules stated in the CSAS document.
  3. Once opted for the Supernumerary quota, you agree to relinquish all claims to the seat/s allocated to you through regular allocations, if any.
  4. You agree to abide by all rules and regulations of the University of Delhi concerning admissions and seat allocation.
